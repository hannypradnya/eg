Pengantar Filsafat & Sad Darśana

Kata Darśana berasal dari urat kata 'drs' yang artinya melihat atau mengalami[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span). Filsafat Hindu (Sad Darśana) berakar kuat pada otoritas Weda[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span)[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span). Klasifikasi filsafat India terbagi atas Astika (mengakui otoritas Weda) dan Nastika (menolak Weda seperti Carwaka, Jaina, Buddha)[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span).

1. Nyaya Darśana

Didirikan oleh Rsi Gautama (Aksapada)[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span). Menekankan logika dan epistemologi melalui Catur Pramana (Pratyaksa, Anumana, Upamana, Sabda)[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span)[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span). Tujuan akhir: Apawarga[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span)[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span).

2. Waiśesika Darśana

Didirikan oleh Rsi Kanada[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span). Mengajarkan pluralisme atomik dengan 7 kategori realitas (Padartha: Drawya, Guna, Karma, Samanya, Visesa, Samawaya, Abhava)[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span)[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span).

3. Sāmkhya Darśana

Didirikan oleh Sri Kapila Muni[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span). Mengajarkan dualisme Purusa (roh) dan Prakrti (materi) beserta 25 Tattwas[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span)[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span), digerakkan oleh Triguna (Sattwa, Rajas, Tamas)[span_26](start_span)[span_26](end_span)[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span).

4. Yoga Darśana

Didirikan oleh Maharsi Patanjali[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span). "Yogas Citta Wrtti Nirodah"[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span). Mengembangkan Astangga Yoga (Yama, Niyama, Asana, Pranayama, Pratyahara, Dharana, Dhyana, Samadhi)[span_30](start_span)[span_30](end_span)[span_31](start_span)[span_31](end_span).
